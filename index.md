# Anitide

![Anitide](logo.png)

Anitide is a desktop app for tracking anime. It brings your watchlist, schedule, seasonal charts and notifications into one place and can keep your MyAnimeList list in sync.

## What it does

- A watchlist with the usual lists: Watching, Completed, On Hold, Dropped, Plan to watch and Re-watching.
- Notifications when a new episode of something you follow comes out.
- Seasonal and schedule views for finding what to watch next.
- Optional synchronization with your MyAnimeList account: status, score and watched episodes, in either direction, and only for your own anime list.

## MyAnimeList

Anitide connects to MyAnimeList through the official MyAnimeList API. You sign in on the MyAnimeList website and choose what is allowed; Anitide never sees your password. The connection can be removed at any time, in the app and on MyAnimeList (Account Settings, API).

How Anitide treats your data is described in the [privacy policy](privacy).

Anitide is a personal, non-commercial project and is not affiliated with MyAnimeList.
