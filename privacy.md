# Anitide privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Anitide is a desktop app. It does not have its own server or accounts, and it does not collect data about you.

## What is stored, and where

Your watchlist, settings and notifications are stored only on your own computer. Nothing of this is sent to the author of the app or to any third party.

## MyAnimeList

If you choose to connect your MyAnimeList account, Anitide uses the official MyAnimeList API to:

- read your anime list (titles, list status, score, watched episodes, dates), and
- update your anime list when you ask it to, or when you turn on automatic sync.

The sign-in happens on the MyAnimeList website. Anitide does not receive or store your password. The access tokens that MyAnimeList gives the app are kept encrypted on your computer and are used only to talk to MyAnimeList. You can disconnect at any time in the app, and revoke access on MyAnimeList under Account Settings, API.

## Other services

To show titles, posters and release information, the app contacts public anime services from your computer. These requests do not include your MyAnimeList data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the repository where it is published.
